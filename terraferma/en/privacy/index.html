Privacy Policy

Terraferma · November 24, 2026 · Italiano · Deutsch · Français · Español

Terraferma has no accounts and no servers, and its developer receives none of your data. Your games, progress and settings stay on your device. Something leaves it only if you use Game Center or make a purchase, and then it goes through Apple.

What the app does

Terraferma is a strategy game: you start from a nation on the map and expand against other nations led by the computer or by other players. It runs on iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Where your data lives

The name you choose, your colour and building style, language, sound, achievements, daily challenge results and other settings are stored in the app's preferences on your device. There is no sign-up and no profile on our side. If you delete the app, this data goes with it.

Game Center

Game Center is Apple's gaming service. If you are signed in to Game Center on your device:

  • Achievements and leaderboard: the achievements you earn and your daily challenge score are sent to Game Center, which shows them to you and, depending on your Game Center settings, to your friends.
  • "Friends" and "Worldwide" matches: Game Center connects the players. During a match the other players receive your name (the one you chose, or your Game Center name), your colour and your moves. This data travels through Game Center and reaches only the players in the same match, not us.

Game Center is run by Apple under its privacy policy. Without Game Center you can play "Solo" with no limits.

Purchases

"Terraferma Full" is bought through the App Store. Apple handles the payment: we never see your payment details or your Apple Account. The app only asks Apple whether the purchase was made, to unlock the content.

Data collected

None. We do not collect personal data and we receive no usage statistics. If you chose to share analytics with developers in your device settings, Apple may show us anonymous, aggregated statistics and crash reports, with nothing that identifies you.

The app contains no analytics or tracking tools, shows no ads, does not use the advertising identifier and shares nothing with advertisers.

Children

Terraferma collects no data from anyone, children included. Game Center has its own age rules and controls, managed from Screen Time.

Your rights

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) you have rights of access, rectification and erasure towards whoever processes your data. We process no personal data, so there is nothing for us to show you or delete. For Game Center and purchase data, your Apple Account applies.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
