Before you write

How to play

From the menu, "How to play" explains expansion, attacks, ships, railways, bombs and alliances, with tips for your first games.

"Friends" or "Worldwide" finds no one

Matches with other players use Game Center: sign in from your device settings, under Game Center, and check your internet connection. To play with friends, one player creates the room and the others join with its code. Everyone needs the latest version of Terraferma.

I bought "Terraferma Full" but it is still locked

Open the shop from the menu and tap "Restore purchases", using the same Apple Account you bought with. The purchase also works with Family Sharing.

Changing language, music or sounds

From the menu, open Settings.

Suggest a feature

Write to the same address: the most requested features move to the top of the list.
