Privacy Policy

DeckSleeve · updated 3 October 2026 · Italiano

DeckSleeve has no accounts and no servers, and its developer receives none of your data. The cards you own, what you paid for them, the sets you follow and the cards you watch stay on your device and nowhere else. Card photos never leave your phone. What follows lists every moment the app talks to the outside world and exactly what leaves it.

What the app does

You point the camera at a trading card — Pokémon, Magic: The Gathering, Yu-Gi-Oh!, One Piece, Dragon Ball Super — and the app recognises it, adds it to your collection and tells you what it is worth today. It keeps track of sets, of your collection's value over time and of the prices of cards you want to watch.

Where your data lives

Everything you enter — cards, quantities, condition, language, price paid, notes, followed sets, watched cards and price thresholds — is stored in the app's database on your device, protected by the system's encryption. So are the downloaded catalogues, the history of prices seen and the card images already shown, so they are not downloaded twice.

There is no sign-up and no profile. Your data is included in your device backups like everything else, and deleting the app deletes it. Today the app does not use iCloud: if sync ever arrives, it will stay off until you turn it on, and this page will say so first.

The camera

The camera is used only to read cards. Recognition happens on the device, with Apple's built-in tools: no photo or frame is uploaded anywhere.

The last few frames read are kept briefly in a temporary folder of the app, to show your photo next to the catalogue picture when the app asks you to confirm a card. The folder is emptied every time you reopen the scanner, and the system may empty it at any time.

If you choose to read a photo from your library, the app uses the system picker, which hands over only the photo you picked: the app has no access to the rest of your library.

What leaves the device, and to whom

To recognise cards and price them, the app downloads catalogues and prices from public sources. These requests contain nothing that identifies you: no name, no email, no device identifier, no list of what you own. They contain only what is needed to find a card:

  • the sets and cards to download or update in the catalogue;
  • the cards to price, by their code — one at a time or in groups, without saying they are yours;
  • the text read off a card — name, number, set code — when the card is not in the phone's catalogue and has to be looked up at the source. Never the photo;
  • what you type in search, when the card searched for is not already on the phone;
  • card images, downloaded from the addresses the catalogue gives.

The sources are TCGdex and pokemontcg.io (Pokémon), Scryfall (Magic), YGOPRODeck (Yu-Gi-Oh!), TCGCSV (One Piece, Dragon Ball and sealed products), and the public price guides of Cardmarket. Like any internet service, they see the IP address a request comes from and handle it under their own policies. The app identifies itself to them by its name and version, nothing else.

Background prices and notifications

If you turn notifications on, the app occasionally — even when closed — asks the same sources for the prices of cards you watch or own, and compares the result with your thresholds on the device. Notifications are local: the phone prepares and shows them, and no server sends them, because there is no server. Turn them off in the app under Profile → Notifications, or in the system settings.

Widgets and the Control Center button

The Home Screen widgets show your collection's value and the cards moving it. The app writes that summary to a protected area of the device that only DeckSleeve and its widgets can read; it never leaves the device. With «Hide amounts» on, the widgets show no figures. The Control Center and Lock Screen button only opens the app on the scanner: it reads and sends nothing.

Import and export

Export creates a CSV file of your cards and hands it to the system share sheet: where it goes next is up to you. Import reads only the file you pick in the system picker.

Purchases

DeckSleeve Pro — a monthly or yearly subscription, or a one-time purchase — goes through Apple. We see only whether a purchase is active on the device: not who you are, not how you paid. Restoring purchases talks to Apple, not to us. Subscriptions are cancelled from your Apple account settings.

Data collected

None. We collect no personal data, keep no user database, and receive no usage statistics or crash reports.

The app contains no analytics or tracking tools, shows no ads, does not use the device's advertising identifier and shares nothing with advertisers or brokers.

Retention and deletion

We keep nothing, because nothing reaches us. Your data stays on your device for as long as you decide. You can remove single cards from your collection, remove a game and its catalogue under Profile → Games and catalogue, or delete the app, which erases everything. There is no need to ask us: we have no copy.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children. Collecting data from no one, it collects none from them.

Your rights

The EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) gives you rights of access, rectification, portability and erasure towards whoever processes your data. Here the answer is structural: we process no personal data, so there is nothing for us to show you or erase. The data you entered is already entirely in your hands, and the CSV export gives it to you in a portable file.
