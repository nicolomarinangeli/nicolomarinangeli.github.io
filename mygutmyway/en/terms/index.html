My Gut, My Way · updated 5 November 2026 · Italiano

My Gut, My Way is not a medical device. It is not a medical device under Regulation (EU) 2017/745 or any similar law, and it is not intended to diagnose, prevent, monitor, treat or alleviate any disease or condition. It is a cooking and personal note-taking app.

What it's for

The app helps you: find recipes that leave out the foods you choose to avoid; keep a personal diary of meals, drinks, symptoms and habits; organize your weekly menu, shopping and pantry; and read labels and menus to look for the words you set. It is meant for adults.

What it doesn't do

  • It doesn't diagnose and doesn't tell you what causes your symptoms. Diary “observations” only describe coincidences in what you noted: they are not proof and not advice.
  • It doesn't prescribe diets or treatments and doesn't replace a doctor, a dietitian or any other health professional.
  • Recipes don't cure, prevent or relieve any condition. They are general cooking information.
  • The low FODMAP path is only a note-taking tool, for people already following that path with a professional.

Allergies and intolerances

Recipes, swaps and label or menu checks can contain mistakes or miss an ingredient. Always read the label yourself and ask the restaurant. If you have a severe allergy, follow your allergist's advice. Allergies and intolerances should be confirmed by a doctor; before cutting out gluten, ask your doctor whether to be tested for celiac disease.

When to see a doctor

Trouble swallowing, persistent vomiting, blood in your stool or black stools, weight loss without trying, severe pain, fever, symptoms that wake you at night, or new symptoms after age 50 always need a doctor, whatever the cause. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.
