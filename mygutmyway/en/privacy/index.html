Privacy Policy

My Gut, My Way · updated 8 November 2026 · Italiano

My Gut, My Way has no accounts and no servers, and its developer receives none of your data. Your meal and symptom diary, the foods you avoid, your recipes and your pantry stay on your iPhone. Below is every moment the app talks to the outside world, and exactly what leaves your hands.

What the app does

It suggests recipes that respect the foods you say you avoid, keeps a diary of meals, drinks, symptoms and other factors, plans the week's menu and shopping list, and helps you check labels and menus. It is not a medical device: it doesn't diagnose and doesn't treat. See the Terms of Use.

Where your data lives

Everything you enter — profile, foods to avoid, diary, saved recipes, menu, shopping list, pantry, reminders — is stored in the app's database on your iPhone, protected by the system's encryption. There is no sign-up, no sync with servers of ours and no iCloud.

Your diary contains health data. That's exactly why it never leaves the phone: it doesn't come to us, and it isn't sent to any AI assistant, not even one you connect yourself.

Your data is included in your iPhone backup, if you have backups turned on, like the rest of the phone. If you delete the app, the data goes with it.

Camera and photos

The camera is used to read food labels, restaurant menus and your pantry. Text is read on your iPhone, with Apple's built-in tools. If you pick a photo from your library, the system picker hands the app only the photo you choose.

The recipe assistant

  • Apple Intelligence (the default): recipe ideas are created on your iPhone. Nothing leaves it.
  • Your own ChatGPT (OpenAI), Gemini (Google) or Claude (Anthropic) key: only if you add one, and after asking for your consent. That company receives only the recipe request — meal, time, cuisine, foods to leave out, the dish you check, what you type — and, if you use “Cook from my fridge”, the fridge photo. Never your diary, symptoms or notes. The request goes straight from your iPhone to that company, not through us, and its privacy policy applies. Your key is kept in the iPhone Keychain.

Wikipedia, Wikibooks and Open Food Facts

When you check a dish with “Should I eat it?”, the app first looks in the dish book it carries: the main ingredients of thousands of dishes, taken from Wikipedia, and many recipes with their amounts, taken from Wikibooks, all kept on your iPhone. The app sends nothing to Wikibooks. When there's a connection, it sends Wikipedia (Wikimedia Foundation) only the dish name, to read the page that describes it. It never sends your diary, symptoms, the foods you avoid or anything that identifies you. Offline, the check still works with the dish book alone.

When you scan a product's barcode, the app sends Open Food Facts only that code, to read the product's ingredients and allergens.

Either way the request goes straight from your iPhone to that service, not through us, and its privacy policy applies (Wikimedia, Open Food Facts).

Reminders

Reminders are local notifications: your iPhone prepares and shows them, no server sends them. Turn them off in the app or in system Settings.

Exports

The PDF summary for your doctor or dietitian and the CSV file of your diary are created on your iPhone and handed to the system share sheet. Where they go next is up to you.

Purchases

The Plus subscription goes through the App Store. We only see whether a purchase is active on the iPhone: not who you are, not how you paid. Cancel it in Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions.

Data collected

None. We don't collect personal data, have no user database and receive no usage statistics. The app contains no analytics or tracking tools, shows no ads and doesn't use the advertising identifier. If you chose to share analytics with developers in your iPhone settings, Apple may show us anonymous, aggregated crash reports.

Deletion

You can delete everything at any time from Me → Delete all my data, or by deleting the app. There's no need to ask us: we don't have a copy.

Children

The app is meant for adults and collects no data from anyone, so none from children either.

Your rights

The EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) gives you rights of access, rectification, portability and erasure towards whoever processes your data. We process no personal data: what you enter is already entirely in your hands, and the CSV export gives it to you in a portable file. For data you send to OpenAI, Google or Anthropic with your own key, their policies apply.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
