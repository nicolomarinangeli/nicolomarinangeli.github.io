Frequently asked questions

Where does my data go?
It stays on your iPhone. We have no servers and receive nothing. Details in the Privacy Policy.

How do I delete everything?
Me → Delete all my data. Or delete the app.

How do I share my notes with my doctor?
Me → Export for your doctor or dietitian: it creates a PDF summary, or a CSV file with your whole diary.

Why isn't a recipe suggested to me?
It contains a food you set in Me → My foods. In Recipes you can see how many recipes were hidden, and why.

The recipe assistant doesn't work.
Apple Intelligence needs a compatible iPhone with Apple Intelligence turned on in Settings. Otherwise you can add your own ChatGPT, Gemini or Claude key in Me → Recipe assistant. Without an assistant, all curated recipes stay available.

How do I cancel Plus?
iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → My Gut, My Way. Or in the app, Me → Manage subscription. If you cancel during the trial, you aren't charged.

I have a new iPhone and Plus is missing.
Open Plus and tap “Restore purchases”, with the same Apple Account.

I have a new iPhone and my diary is missing.
Your diary travels with your iPhone backup (iCloud or computer). Restore the backup on the new phone.
