Privacy Policy

PetCare Family · updated November 2, 2026 · Italiano · Español · Deutsch · Français

PetCare Family has no accounts and no servers of ours. What you write about your pets stays on your iPhone and, if you choose to share it with your family, in iCloud: nothing ever reaches the developer. Below is where your data lives and the few moments when something leaves the app — always because you chose to.

What the app does

It keeps a folder for each pet — profile, reminders, health diary, documents, expenses, emergency card — and a log of who did what and when, so the family knows whether the pill has already been given.

Where your data lives

Pets, activities, who did them, health entries, weights, expenses, documents, photos, people's names and vet contacts are stored in the app's database on your iPhone, protected by the system's encryption. There is no sign-up. They are included in your iPhone backup — iCloud or computer — like the rest of the phone, if backups are on.

The names you add under Family are only used to sign completed activities: they are not accounts, they receive nothing and nobody contacts them.

Sharing with the family on iCloud

Sharing is off until you turn it on. If you tap “Invite your family”, the app uses Apple's iCloud (CloudKit): pets, activities and records are kept in the private iCloud of whoever sent the invitation and are shared only with the people who accept it, each on their own iPhone and with their own Apple Account. The data is stored by Apple and covered by Apple's privacy policy; the developer has no access to it and cannot see who is in your family. It uses the iCloud storage of whoever sent the invitation.

Whoever sent the invitation can stop sharing at any time, and whoever accepted it can leave the family: in both cases each iPhone keeps a copy of what it had, and changes stop travelling.

Reading a prescription

When you scan a prescription, the text is read on your iPhone, with Apple's text recognition and, where available, Apple Intelligence running on the device. The image and the text never leave the iPhone, and nothing is saved until you have checked it.

Camera, photos and files

  • The camera is only used to scan documents — prescriptions, vaccination booklets, reports — with the system scanner. Scans stay in the app.
  • Photos are chosen with the system picker, which gives the app only the ones you select: it has no access to the rest of your library.
  • Files (for example a PDF from the vet) are chosen with the document picker: the app keeps a copy and reads nothing else.

Reminders, Siri and widgets

Notifications are local: your iPhone prepares and shows them, no server sends them. You can turn them off in Settings, under PetCare. Siri, Shortcuts and widgets read the same data on the iPhone itself.

When something leaves the app

Only when you do it:

  • sharing the emergency card, the vet summary, the pet sitter instructions or the lost pet poster, which the app creates on your iPhone and hands to the system share sheet. Where it goes next is up to you;
  • saving a backup or exporting expenses as a spreadsheet: files you put wherever you like — in Files, on iCloud Drive or elsewhere;
  • adding an event to your calendar through the system sheet: the app does not read your calendars;
  • tapping a phone number of a contact, which opens the Phone app;
  • opening a link such as this page, which opens in your browser.

Data collected

None. We don't collect personal data, we have no user database, and we receive no usage statistics or crash reports. The app has no analytics or tracking tools, shows no ads, does not use the advertising identifier and shares nothing with anyone. Family sharing goes through your own Apple Account, not through us.

Retention and deletion

We keep nothing, because nothing reaches us. You can delete a pet with everything about it from its page → Edit → Delete pet; in a shared family it disappears for everyone. Deleting the app removes everything on that iPhone; what is shared with the family stays in the iCloud of whoever sent the invitation until they stop sharing. There's no need to ask us: we have no copy.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone, so none from them either.
