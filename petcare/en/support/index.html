PetCare Family has no accounts and no servers of ours: your data lives on your iPhone and, if you share it, in your family's iCloud. Nobody can look at it remotely. To get help, just write and describe what's happening.

Frequently asked questions

Reminders don't arrive

Turn them on from Family → Reminders; the first time, iOS asks for permission. If you denied it, turn them back on in Settings, under PetCare. With a Focus mode on they may arrive silently.

How do I record who did an activity?

Tapping the circle signs the activity with the name chosen in Family → “Who uses this iPhone?”. To sign it with another name, swipe the activity to the left or touch and hold it.

Can several people use the app on different phones?

Yes. In Family, under “Family on iCloud”, tap “Invite your family” and send the invitation. Whoever accepts it on their own iPhone sees the same pets, activities and records, and when someone marks an activity as done it leaves the others' list. Everyone needs to be signed in to iCloud. Then, on each iPhone, choose “Who uses this iPhone?” so activities are signed with the right name.

Changes don't reach the other iPhone

Check that both iPhones are online and signed in to iCloud, then open the app: changes usually arrive within a few seconds. If the app says iCloud is full, whoever sent the invitation needs to free up space in their iCloud.

How do I bring the records to the vet?

On the pet's page, “For the vet” creates a PDF with vaccinations, planned care (and when it was last given), recent history, weight, documents and contacts, to show or send.

I'm going away: what do I leave the pet sitter?

On the pet's page, “For the pet sitter” creates a sheet with the daily routine and a box to tick for each of the next seven days, what is coming up and who to call. The “Emergency card” puts allergies, microchip and vet on a single page.

How do we split the chores in the family?

Open an activity and use “Who takes care of it” to assign it to someone: the name shows in Today, in the Calendar and in the reminder.

I lost my data

Your data lives on the iPhone and is included in its backups — iCloud or computer — like everything else: restoring the backup brings it back. You can also save everything in a single file from Family → Backup → “Save a backup” and keep it in Files or on iCloud Drive; “Restore a backup” adds what is missing and never deletes anything. If you share with your family, the other iPhones keep a copy too.
