Privacy Policy

Syllabus Planner · TikTok publisher · updated 10 October 2026

This policy covers one small tool: the TikTok publisher that the developer of Syllabus Planner uses to send promotional videos for the app to its own TikTok account. It is not a service for the public and it does not collect data from visitors.

Who is responsible

Nicolò Marinangeli, independent developer, Italy. Contact: nicolom.dev.supporto@gmail.com.

What the tool is

A web page plus a small program that runs on the developer's own computer. The page lets the developer connect a TikTok account with TikTok Login Kit and send a video to that account's TikTok drafts through the Content Posting API (upload to inbox). Nothing is published automatically: the caption and the publish button stay in the TikTok app.

The Syllabus Planner iOS app itself does not connect to TikTok and sends nothing to TikTok. Its own privacy policy is here.

Data involved

  • From TikTok, when an account is connected (scope user.info.basic): the account's open ID, display name and profile picture address, shown on screen so the developer can see which account is connected.
  • Access and refresh tokens issued by TikTok, needed to call the API on the account's behalf.
  • The video file the developer chooses (scope video.upload), sent straight to TikTok.

No other TikTok data is requested, and data about other TikTok users is never read.

Where it is kept

Tokens are stored in a file on the developer's own computer, readable only by that user. There is no server, no database, no analytics, no advertising, no cookies and no sharing with third parties. The web page is static: it stores nothing and talks only to the program on the same computer and to TikTok. Videos are not kept by the tool after they are sent.

Keeping and deleting

Tokens are kept until the account is disconnected. The “Disconnect” button revokes the access at TikTok and deletes the local token file. The access can also be removed from the TikTok app, in the settings of the account, where connected apps are listed. For anything else — including a request to delete data — write to the address above.

TikTok

What TikTok does with data on its side is covered by TikTok's own privacy policy. This tool is not affiliated with or endorsed by TikTok.

Children

The tool is not aimed at children and is not offered to anyone but its developer.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
