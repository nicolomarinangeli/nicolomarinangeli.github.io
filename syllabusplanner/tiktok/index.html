TikTok publisher

Syllabus Planner · promotional videos for the app, sent to its own TikTok account

A small personal tool: connect the app's TikTok account with TikTok Login, pick a promotional video, and send it to TikTok as a draft. Nothing is published automatically — the video waits in the TikTok app, where you add the caption and publish it yourself.

The local helper is not running

This page talks to a small program on your own computer. Start it and reload:

node Scripts/tiktok/server.mjs

1 · Connect TikTok

You will be sent to TikTok to authorize two things: reading the basic profile (user.info.basic) and uploading a video as a draft (video.upload).


Connected to TikTok

2 · Send a video to your TikTok drafts

How it works

  1. Login Kit asks TikTok to confirm which account is being connected.
  2. The video goes from your computer to TikTok through the Content Posting API (upload to inbox).
  3. TikTok notifies the account in the TikTok app; the caption and the publish button are there.
