Syllabus Planner

Study planner for college and university exams · iPhone, iPad and Mac · Italiano

Syllabus Planner is a study planner app for iPhone, iPad and Mac that builds your study plan for college and university exams and tells you what to study today. Add your exams, the books or chapters they cover and the hours you have each week: it works backwards from your exam dates and splits the material into days. Miss a day or move faster than expected, and the plan rebuilds itself. Free for one exam.

Download on the App Store

How it works

  1. Add your exams. By hand, or from a photo or PDF of the syllabus: it picks out subjects, exam dates, credits and chapters and asks you to confirm them.
  2. Say how much time you have. Hours per week and, if you like, your lecture timetable: study moves around it.
  3. Every day you know what to do. The Today screen says which pages, from which subject, for how long.
  4. The plan adapts. The Focus timer logs the minutes you really spent, so the app learns your pace instead of trusting your first estimates.

Features

  • A plan that works backwards from exam dates, page by page, recalculated whenever something changes.
  • When you fall behind it says so and offers three ways back, with before-and-after numbers.
  • AI tutor that quizzes you on your notes and grades the answers, or explains a topic.
  • Spaced review after 1, 3, 7 and 14 days, and a mock exam a few days before the real one.
  • Focus timer in 25 and 5-minute cycles, on the Lock Screen too.
  • Calendar and exam countdown for every exam.
  • The same plan on iPhone, iPad and Mac, synced through your iCloud.

Who it's for

College and university students with several exams in the same session and books or chapters to split into days. It also works for finals, bar or board exams, certifications and revision for school exams: all it needs is a date and the material to study.

FAQ

What is a good app to plan studying for college exams?

Syllabus Planner, for iPhone, iPad and Mac, is built for this: add your exams, the books or chapters they cover and the hours you have each week, and it builds the plan backwards from your exam dates. Every day it tells you which pages to study, from which subject, for how long.

What happens if I miss a study day?

The plan rebuilds itself over the days left. If you fall behind, the app says so and offers three ways back: spread the backlog over every remaining day, clear it in the next few days, or keep your days as they are. Each option shows the real before-and-after numbers.

How much does Syllabus Planner cost?

It is free for one exam. For more exams, the full plan costs, on the US App Store, $8.99 per semester, $12.99 per year with a free first week, or $34.99 once, for good. No ads.

Can I upload my syllabus instead of typing it?

Yes. From a photo or a PDF of the course outline the app picks out subjects, exam dates, credits and chapters, and asks you to confirm each one. The reading happens on your device: the document is not sent to any server.

How does the AI tutor work?

It quizzes you on the notes in your notebook and grades your answers, or explains a topic. On devices with Apple Intelligence it runs on Apple's on-device model, with no keys. Otherwise you can connect an AI service with your own key. Any change it proposes to your plan, you apply yourself.

Where is my data stored?

On your device and in your own iCloud, to sync iPhone, iPad and Mac. There is no account to create, no developer server and no tracking.

Which languages does it support?

English, Italian, French, German and Spanish.
