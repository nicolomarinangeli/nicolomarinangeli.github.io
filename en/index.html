Rialto

Spending, budget and net worth, for iPhone and Apple Watch

Pay with Apple Pay and the expense is already logged.

On the right account, with the category worked out, without opening the app. Your budget starts on payday, and accounts, cards, ETFs and cash sit on one screen.

Download on the App Store

Free, with an optional Rialto Pro. Requires iOS 26 or later. In English, Italian, Spanish, German and French.

What it does

  • Apple Pay purchases log themselves, through a Shortcuts automation you set up once in three minutes
  • The month starts on payday, not on the first: you see what is left until the next salary
  • ETFs, shares, bonds, crypto and metals with prices that update, and coupons that credit themselves
  • Bank statements come in from CSV, Excel, OpenDocument or Numbers
  • On Apple Watch, what is left to spend, and cash goes in with a turn of the Digital Crown

Where your data lives

  • On your phone, and in your own iCloud if you turn sync on
  • No account to create, no Rialto server: the developer never receives a single number
  • The AI coach, if you want it, runs on the device with Apple Intelligence
