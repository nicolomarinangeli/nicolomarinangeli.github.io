Support

Marea · updated 19 November 2026 · Italiano

Marea has no accounts and no servers: nobody can look at your data remotely, not even its developer. To get help, just write and describe what is happening.

Write

For any problem, question or request: nicolom.dev.supporto@gmail.com

I reply within a few days. When reporting a bug, it helps to know your iPhone model, your iOS version, what you were doing and what happened instead. Please don't send cycle data or screenshots with personal details unless they are needed.

Before writing: things that sort themselves out

The predictions look wrong

At first Marea uses the lengths set under Settings › Your cycle. Once at least two cycles are logged it uses yours. The more days you log, the closer the estimates get: a skipped or half-logged cycle moves them. If your periods started recently, "My cycle started recently" widens the ranges.

"Ask Marea" says Apple Intelligence is missing

Apple Intelligence works only on iPhones that support it, once it is turned on under Settings › Apple Intelligence & Siri. Right after turning it on, the model may take a while to download. Instead, you can use Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini with a key from your own account, under Settings › Assistant.

The assistant with a key does not answer

Check that the whole key was pasted and that your account with the service has credit or an active plan: questions are billed to that account. An internet connection is needed.

Reminders do not arrive

Turn them on under Settings › Reminders; the first time, the system asks for permission. If you denied it, turn it back on in the system settings under Marea. Focus modes can also silence them.

I lost my data

Your data lives only on the iPhone and is included in its backups, to iCloud or to a computer, like everything else. There is no copy anywhere else. To be safe, use "Export a backup" under Settings › Your data now and then: it is a file you can keep wherever you like and bring back with "Import from file…".

Coming from another app

"Import from file…" reads tables exported by many cycle apps, with your period dates and, when present, the flow. Days already logged in Marea stay as they are.
