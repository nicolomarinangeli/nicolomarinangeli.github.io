Privacy Policy

Marea · updated 19 November 2026 · Italiano

Marea has no accounts and no servers, and its developer receives none of your data. Your cycle, the days you log and your notes live on your iPhone. Something leaves the phone only when you decide so: a backup or a report you share, or a question to the assistant asked through an outside service with your own key. What follows explains each case.

What the app does

Marea is a menstrual cycle diary: you log your periods, flow, symptoms, mood, energy and, if you want, fertility signs. From what you log it estimates the phases, your next period, ovulation and the fertile window. It is available for iPhone only.

Marea is not a medical device and not a contraceptive: its estimates are not enough to prevent a pregnancy or to diagnose anything. Talk to a doctor about any health concern.

Where your data lives

Everything you log — period days, flow, symptoms and pain, mood, energy, cervical fluid and its color, ovulation tests, temperature, intimacy and notes — is stored in the app's database on your iPhone, protected by the system's encryption. Settings (cycle lengths, goals, reminders, what to show) are kept in the app's preferences, also on the phone.

There is no sign-up and no profile. Marea does not use iCloud: your data is not synced to other devices. It is included in your iPhone's backup, to iCloud or to a computer, if you have one turned on, like any other app's data. If you delete the app, the data goes with it.

Lock and cover

With the lock turned on, under Settings › Privacy, Marea opens only after Face ID, Touch ID or your iPhone passcode. iOS does the recognition: the app only receives a yes or a no, never your face or your fingerprint. "Cover in the app list" blurs Marea when you switch between apps, so its content does not show in the preview.

Reminders

Reminders are local notifications: your iPhone prepares and shows them, no server sends them, because there is no server. With "Discreet text" on, they never mention periods or fertility on the lock screen. Turn them off under Settings › Reminders or in the system settings.

Widgets

The widgets show your cycle day, the phase and how long until your next period. The app writes that summary to a protected area of the device that only Marea and its widgets can read. A period start logged from the widget passes to the app the same way. It never leaves the phone.

Backups, imports and the doctor report

  • "Export a backup", under Settings › Your data, creates a JSON file with every logged day and your cycle settings, and saves it wherever you choose with the system file picker: Files, iCloud Drive or another place. Where it goes next is up to you. The file is not encrypted: keep it somewhere safe.
  • "Import from file…" reads only the file you pick: a Marea backup or a table exported from another app.
  • The doctor report is a PDF created on the phone. You share it yourself, with the system share sheet, with whoever you like. The working copy stays in the app's temporary folder, which the system clears.

The "Ask Marea" assistant

You can ask an assistant questions about your cycle. You choose who answers, under Settings › Assistant:

  • Apple Intelligence, the default. Apple's model runs on your iPhone: your questions and your cycle summary never leave the phone.
  • Claude (Anthropic), ChatGPT (OpenAI) or Gemini (Google), with an API key from your own account with that service. These are third-party services that work over the internet.

Before sending anything to one of these services, Marea asks for your explicit permission, service by service. Without your yes nothing is sent, and even after it nothing is sent until you ask a question. You can withdraw the permission at any time, on the same page.

With your permission, your questions and the conversation go straight from your iPhone to the chosen service, with your key. Marea has no server and sees nothing. The service handles them under its own rules: Anthropic's privacy policy, OpenAI's privacy policy, Google's Gemini API terms.

The cycle summary

"Include my cycle summary" is off until you turn it on, and it is turned on for one service at a time: on for one does not count for another chosen later. When it is on, every question also carries:

  • your goals in the app;
  • your cycle dates, lengths and averages;
  • the estimated ovulation and fertile window;
  • the last weeks of your diary: flow, symptoms, mood, energy, cervical fluid and its color, ovulation tests, temperature.

With "Hide fertility and intimacy" on, fertility details stay out. Notes and intimacy are never sent. When the summary is off, only your questions and the conversation are sent.

Your key and the list of models

Your key is kept in the iPhone's Keychain, readable only while the phone is unlocked, only on that device and outside your backups. You can remove it at any time. Once a week, when you ask something, Marea asks the chosen service for its list of available models, with your key, so it can use the newest one: that request carries no cycle data.

Health data

Under European law, cycle data is data concerning health, a special category of personal data. We do not process it: it stays on your phone. If you choose to send it to an AI service, you do so with your explicit consent, and that service handles it under its own policy. Deleting it there follows that service's rules and your account with it.

Data collected

None. We collect no personal data, have no user database and receive no usage statistics. If you chose in your iPhone's settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may show us anonymous, aggregated statistics and crash reports for the app, with nothing that identifies you.

The app contains no analytics or tracking tools, shows no ads, does not use the device's advertising identifier and shares nothing with advertisers or data brokers.

Retention and deletion

We keep nothing, because nothing reaches us. Your data stays on the phone for as long as you decide. You can delete single days, everything at once with Settings › Your data › "Delete all data", or delete the app. Backup files you exported stay where you put them: delete them there. There is no need to ask us: we have no copy.

Children

Marea can also help someone whose periods started recently: that is why everything about fertility and intimacy can be hidden. Since it collects data from no one, it collects none from children either. The assistant's outside services have their own age rules: read them before creating a key.

Your rights

The EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) gives you rights of access, rectification, portability and erasure towards whoever processes your data. Here the answer is structural: we process no personal data, so there is nothing for us to show you or delete. The data you entered is already entirely in your hands, and a backup gives it to you in a portable file.
